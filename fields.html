Areas of Expertise

Main Fields of Studies & Research

The Centre focuses on multidisciplinary research areas that contribute to institutional development, policy improvement, and sustainable change.

01

Political & Constitutional Affairs

Focuses on achieving consensus around a legitimate constitutional framework, promoting national reconciliation mechanisms, and implementing local governance while preserving national unity. It also explores strengthening the separation of powers, restoring national sovereignty, increasing the participation of youth and women in political life, developing political parties and civil society institutions, and addressing regional and international influences.

02

Economic Affairs

Aims to develop strategic visions for transforming Libya from an oil-dependent rentier economy into a diversified and productive economy, while ensuring transparent and equitable management of natural resources. It also addresses financial governance, restructuring public institutions into competitive entities, supporting the private sector and SMEs, developing infrastructure, and strengthening financial stability and food security.

03

Social Affairs

Concerned with strengthening a unified national identity and promoting dialogue and tolerance, while addressing violence and extremism and providing social protection for vulnerable groups. It also focuses on improving education and healthcare, empowering women, enhancing the role of families and civil society, and studying the impact of digital and environmental transformations on social cohesion.

04

Institutional Affairs

Focuses on building strong and effective state institutions based on good governance, transparency, accountability, and anti-corruption principles. It includes improving public administration, strategic planning, digital government, automation, developing young leadership, and modernizing the legislative and legal framework.

05

Defense & Security Affairs

Addresses security sector reform, restructuring the armed forces and security institutions, developing disarmament, rehabilitation and reintegration strategies for former combatants, establishing a comprehensive national security strategy independent of foreign interference, securing borders, combating organized crime, irregular migration and terrorism, and strengthening security governance and cybersecurity.

The Libyan Centre for Change is dedicated to advancing research, institutional development, and evidence-based policymaking for sustainable national progress.
