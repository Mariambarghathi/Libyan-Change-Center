About The Centre

A Platform for Knowledge, Research & Change

The Libyan Centre for Change was established as an independent research and development institution dedicated to supporting national transformation through research, dialogue, and evidence-based solutions.

The Centre works to bridge the gap between knowledge and practice by producing studies, developing initiatives, and supporting decision makers.

Establishment Framework

  • Research and knowledge production
  • Institutional development support
  • Policy analysis and recommendations
  • Strategic partnerships and cooperation
01

Vision

To become a leading centre for research, innovation, and institutional transformation supporting sustainable development in Libya.

02

Mission

Providing high-quality research, strategic analysis, and practical solutions that contribute to effective governance and positive change.

03

Objectives

Develop knowledge resources, support institutions, encourage dialogue, and contribute to national development initiatives.
