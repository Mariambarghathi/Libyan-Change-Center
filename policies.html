Human Resources Policies

Human resources policies cover four key areas that regulate the Centre's working environment and support efficiency, continuous development, and professional integrity.

1

Recruitment & Appointment

Standards of competence, integrity, and equal opportunity in selecting researchers and staff.

2

Continuous Development

Policies for the regular training of staff and researchers to improve performance quality and develop professional skills.

3

Performance Evaluation & Motivation

Objective performance measurement mechanisms and a reward system that encourages excellence and innovation.

4

Integrity & Professional Ethics

Commitment to research ethics, objectivity, transparency, and the avoidance of conflicts of interest.

Financial & Funding Policies

Financial policies establish clear rules for managing financial resources, ensuring responsible administration, diversified funding sources, and financial sustainability.

1

Funding Sources

Identifying legitimate funding channels, including grants and consultancy services, in accordance with the Centre's objectives and principles.

2

Financial Sustainability

Policies for diversifying resources to ensure the Centre's independence and long-term sustainability.

The Libyan Centre for Change is dedicated to advancing research, institutional development, and evidence-based policymaking for sustainable national progress.
