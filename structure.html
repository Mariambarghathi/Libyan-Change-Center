Organizational Structure

Senior Leadership

Responsible for governance and strategic oversight, while ensuring institutional compliance, integrity, and financial transparency.

01

Board of Trustees

Composed of distinguished national and international figures, the Board is responsible for governance and integrity, appointing the Board of Directors and financial auditor, and approving the budget.

02

Board of Directors

Responsible for strategic management before the Board of Trustees. It develops policies, determines research priorities, appoints the General Director, and approves institutional regulations.

Core Units

Executive Management

Responsible for managing daily operations, coordinating activities, developing capacities, and building local and international relationships.

01

General Director

Represents the Centre before external entities and is responsible for executive management, implementing plans, concluding contracts, and proposing strategies and the budget.

02

Capacity Building & Leadership Development Coordinator

Provides technical consultancy and implements training and qualification programs for state leaders and stakeholders in cooperation with international institutions.

03

Communications & Media Coordinator

Develops media strategies, manages digital platforms, coverage and content, and builds relationships with media organizations.

04

Administrative & Financial Affairs Coordinator

Oversees human resources and logistical services, maintains financial records and accounts, and ensures auditing and compliance with the financial system.

05

International Cooperation Coordinator

Builds partnerships with research centres, universities, and international organizations, and coordinates international events, grants, and memoranda of understanding.

06

Research & Studies Coordinator

Directly supervises research units, applies academic standards, reviews and verifies studies, and updates the research agenda.
