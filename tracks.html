Our Activities

Main Tracks of Work

The Centre implements a range of programs and initiatives that translate research into practical solutions and sustainable impact.

01

Research & Analysis

The main engine of the Centre for producing rigorous knowledge. It includes conducting in-depth field research and studies addressing political, social, and economic issues; preparing policy papers and rapid situation assessment reports to provide practical recommendations to decision-makers; implementing research projects examining the impact of ideologies in Libya; and producing an annual strategic report summarizing key national developments.

02

Dialogue & Engagement

Aims to strengthen the Centre's role as a platform for interaction and consensus-building through Track II diplomacy efforts that build bridges between Libyan stakeholders and regional and international actors. It also includes organizing specialized seminars and workshops that bring experts together with policymakers, as well as holding regular conferences with broad local and international participation to discuss critical issues.

03

Organizational Consulting & Capacity Building

Focuses on empowering national institutions and professionals by providing advisory services in development strategy design across political, economic, and social areas, as well as restructuring and institutional development. It also includes designing and implementing specialized training programs to develop the skills of researchers, leaders, and national stakeholders, equipping them with tools for decision-making and change management.

04

Publishing

Focuses on making knowledge accessible and expanding its impact by publishing research and studies in peer-reviewed journals, books, and policy briefs across different media. It also includes developing a comprehensive reference database on Libyan affairs, activating digital platforms, producing interactive content such as infographics and explanatory videos, and establishing co-publishing partnerships with international research centres and publishing houses.

From Research to Action

The Centre connects knowledge creation with practical implementation through research, partnerships, programs, and strategic initiatives.

Work With Us

The Libyan Centre for Change is dedicated to advancing research, institutional development, and evidence-based policymaking for sustainable national progress.
